Farm trailers

A strong trailer moves more crops, feed, soil and stone per trip and saves tractor hours. We supply twenty-eight tractor-drawn trailer models for every job on the farm.

The range runs from a 1-tonne walk-behind cart to an 11-tonne hydraulic rear dumper, with rear, side and three-way tippers, single- and dual-axle models with impact, hydraulic or air brakes, low flatbeds and 5–7 t equipment transporters.

Questions about farm trailers

What size tractor do I need?

The trailers are sized for tractors from 10 hp up to 150 hp. Tell us your tractor and the loads you carry.

Which way do the tippers tip?

Rear, left, right or all three ways, depending on the model.

Can the trailers be used on public roads?

They are farm trailers. Ask us before ordering if you need one registered for public roads.
