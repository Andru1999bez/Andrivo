Our mission

Built by farmers, for farmers. Our mission is to give every farmer access to the infrastructure and equipment that makes their land productive — sourced directly, priced honestly, with nothing hidden in between.

Why Andrivo exists

Farmers usually pay for a chain of markups they never see.

Andrivo was started by farmers, for farmers. We've felt firsthand what it costs when equipment passes through a factory, an export agent, an importer, and a local distributor — each one adding a markup nobody discloses.

Andrivo removes that chain. We work directly with international manufacturers, manage the shipping, customs and paperwork ourselves, and deliver the equipment straight to your farm, anywhere in Southern Africa.
